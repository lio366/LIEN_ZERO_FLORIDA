Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

TL;DR: We collect only what is necessary to process your lien foreclosures. We do not sell your data. We use encryption. You can delete your data anytime.

1. Introduction

LIENZERO ("we", "us", or "our") operates the LIENZERO mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

By using LIENZERO, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name and Contact: Your name, email address, phone number
  • Business Information: Company name, business address (for Florida mechanics lien requirements)
  • Payment Information: Processed by Google Play Billing (we do not store card details)

2.2 Vehicle & Lien Data

  • Vehicle Information: VIN, make, model, year, license plate
  • Owner Information: Name and last known address of vehicle owner
  • Service Records: Repair invoices, dates of service, amounts owed
  • Lien Documents: Notices, filings, court documents generated through the app

2.3 Automatically Collected Data

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, app version
  • Usage Data: Features used, time spent, crash reports (via Firebase)
  • IP Address: For security and fraud prevention

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and manage automotive lien foreclosures
  • Generate legal notices and court filings
  • Send notifications about case status and deadlines
  • Process payments through Google Play Billing
  • Improve our AI agents and app functionality
  • Comply with Florida legal requirements
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

4. Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard security measures:

  • All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
  • Data encrypted at rest (AES-256)
  • Secure servers hosted on Hetzner (EU/Germany)
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We only share data with:

  • Google Play: For payment processing and app distribution
  • Firebase: For analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications
  • USPS: For certified mail delivery (name and address only)
  • Courts: When filing legal documents (as required by law)
  • Legal Authorities: If required by subpoena or court order

6. Your Rights (Florida & US)

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Export your data in standard format
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics in app settings

7. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • Closed Accounts: Data deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Legal Documents: Retained for 7 years per Florida law requirements
  • Backups: Encrypted backups deleted after 90 days

8. Children's Privacy

LIENZERO is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be notified via email or in-app notification.
