❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the lien process take?
Typically 24-48 hours for document preparation, plus court processing time (varies by county).
Do I need a lawyer?
LIENZERO automates document preparation, but we recommend having a Florida-licensed attorney review all filings. We are not a law firm.
What counties are supported?
All 67 counties in Florida, with optimized workflows for the 20 most populous counties.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled per Google Play policies. Contact us within 48 hours if you experience technical issues.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted with AES-256, transmitted via HTTPS, and stored on secure EU servers.
